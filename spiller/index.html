Spiller!

Pour & Stack

Hold to pour. Release right on the line. Hit it and your glass grows; miss and it shrinks. Then send your score to your friends and watch them try.

Coming soon to Google Play
Follow us! @spiller_game

//How it plays

  • One finger, one tap. Hold to pour, release to stop.
  • Stop right on the line for a Perfect, and the glass grows wider. Miss, and it shrinks. Overflow, and it's game over.
  • Chain Perfects to build a streak. The flow speeds up as your score climbs, so keep your nerve.
  • Beat your best score, climb the Game Center leaderboards, earn 13 achievements and share a picture of your glass.

//Support & FAQ

›Is Spiller! free?

Yes. It's free to play with optional ads. Optional purchases let you remove ads or buy coins; they never change the difficulty.

›How do I remove ads?

Tap the no-ads button on the title screen. It's a one-time purchase that removes the banner and the ads between games. Rewarded videos stay available, only when you choose to watch one.

›How do I restore my purchases?

Open the settings (gear icon, top left of the title screen) and tap Restore purchases.

›Is it on Android?

Spiller! is being tested on Google Play right now. Follow @spiller_game to know when it's out.

›What about my data?

Read the privacy policy. You can change your ad choices anytime in the settings, under Privacy choices.
