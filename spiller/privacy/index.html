Spiller: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Spiller (also listed as “Spiller!”) is a one-tap mobile game for iOS and Android. This policy explains what data the game uses, why, and what choices you have.

Who is responsible

Pierre-Antoine Roy, an individual developer based in Belgium (“I”). Contact: hello@pierreantoineroy.com.

In short

  • There is no account, and I do not ask for your name, e-mail or phone number.
  • Your progress (best score, coins, purchases you made, settings) is stored on your device.
  • Crash reports are collected so I can fix bugs.
  • Usage statistics and personalised ads are only used if you agree in the consent screen shown at first launch. You can change your mind at any time in Settings > Privacy choices.
  • The game is not directed at children.

Data stored on your device

Best score, coin balance, owned items and the selected one, sound and vibration settings, daily bonus date, achievements progress and tutorial progress are saved locally. I do not receive this data. Deleting the app deletes it. During testing versions, game logs (score, duration, per-layer results) may also be written to files on your device; they are not sent anywhere by the game.

Tracking on iOS

If Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt appears, you can refuse. The game works the same either way; ads are then non-personalised.

Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Consent: usage statistics, personalised ads, advertising identifier.
  • Legitimate interest: crash reports and security (keeping the game stable), and basic ad delivery.
  • Contract: completing purchases you request.

Retention

Crash and analytics data are kept by Google for the periods set in Firebase (at most 14 months for analytics events). Local data stays on your device until you delete the app.

International transfers

Google and Apple may process data outside the European Economic Area, relying on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Your choices and rights

  • Open Settings > Privacy choices in the game to change your consent at any time.
  • Reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings.
  • Under the GDPR you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, and object to processing. Because I cannot identify you from the random identifiers, you may need to provide the identifier or device details; write to hello@pierreantoineroy.com.
  • You may complain to your data protection authority; in Belgium, the Data Protection Authority.

Children

Spiller is not directed at children and I do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact me and I will help have it removed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated, and for significant changes the game will ask for your consent again where required.
